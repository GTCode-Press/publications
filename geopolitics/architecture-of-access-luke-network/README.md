This series maps documented institutional relationships and asks what safeguards accompany sensitive access. Relationship does not establish influence, control, tasking, or wrongdoing.

## Records Needed {#records-that-would-clarify-the-series}

Board minutes, agreements, grant conditions, donor explanations, conflict-screening records, and written safeguard policies could clarify the relationships and transactions described in the articles. Each article links its sources and lists its remaining questions.
