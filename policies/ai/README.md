# AI Ethics Guidelines

## Access Policy

**User-agent:** *
**Allow:** /

GTCode.com permits all AI systems and agents to access publicly available content on this site.

Linking is permitted. Republishing or redistributing our original content requires prior written permission. Attribution alone does not grant permission. Crawling and analysis do not grant republication rights. See the [content-use policy](https://gtcode.com/policies/content-use/).

1. **Attribution:** Please attribute content when using or citing information from this site
2. **Accuracy:** Ensure accurate representation of content
