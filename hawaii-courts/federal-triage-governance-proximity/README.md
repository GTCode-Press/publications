I filed a referral with the DOJ Public Integrity Section on July 12, 2025. DOJ acknowledged receipt. No further communication or investigative contact about that referral has been communicated to me.[^receipt] Receipt does not establish whether the matter was assigned, investigated, transferred, or declined.

[The Nod](https://gtcode.com/hawaii-courts/the-nod-visual-allegation/#the-courtroom-sequence) holds the underlying courtroom account. This file follows the federal referral and the records needed to establish its handling.

## Federal Legal Requirements {#federal-jurisdiction}

[18 U.S.C. §242](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/242) requires a willful deprivation of a protected federal right under color of law. It can apply to a state judge; a federal proceeding is not required. Investigators would still need to identify the particular federal right, establish its deprivation and willfulness beyond a reasonable doubt, and assess the fair-warning requirement discussed in [United States v. Lanier](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/520/259/).[^242]

The cutoff is evidence relevant to whether I received a meaningful opportunity to be heard and to the intent surrounding the attempted record statement. Standing alone, it does not establish a §242 violation or willfulness. [Mathews v. Eldridge](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/424/319) supplies a context-dependent due-process standard, not a finding that this interruption constituted a criminal deprivation.

[Section 1513(e)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/1513) presents a separate question only if a person knowingly took harmful action with intent to retaliate for truthful information about a possible federal offense supplied to a qualifying federal law-enforcement officer. My FBI and DEA contacts preceded the hearing.[^prior-reports] That chronology does not establish retaliation. The missing evidence concerns the information supplied, the recipients, the actor’s knowledge, and whether a specific harmful action was taken because of those contacts.

The [Two Questions](https://gtcode.com/hawaii-courts/two-questions-wilson-loo/) develops the hearing evidence and the separate Hawaiʻi false-testimony questions. This referral article does not rely on federal §1622: no federal perjury predicate has been established for the state-court oath.

Assuming the ordinary five-year limitation in [18 U.S.C. §3282](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/3282) applies to the §242 theory, the period for the December 2, 2022 conduct would run to approximately December 2, 2027, subject to applicable exceptions and tolling.

## Evidence Available for Review {#what-non-action-means}

The available investigative steps are concrete: interview the witness and other people present; identify the recording, transcript, sealing order, and access process; obtain the court-file exhibit; and reconstruct the courtroom layout and lines of sight. The text message “I took the acid” was introduced as an exhibit in the hearing.[^exhibit] Its context and evidentiary meaning need to be assessed with the question and answer.

Witness accounts should be tested for specificity, opportunity to observe, consistency with the audio and court file, and independent support. The audio can corroborate or contradict the audible sequence. Access to it does not replace evidence of the visual act.

## Dated Staffing Reports {#the-oversight-vacuum}

Reuters reported on June 9, 2025 that the Public Integrity Section had fallen from more than thirty attorneys to about five, with limits on its authority to bring new cases.[^capacity] An August 28, 2025 congressional oversight letter cited reports that an FBI public-corruption squad was being disbanded.[^capacity] These are dated reports about agency capacity, not a statement of staffing in October 2026. No cited agency record establishes that those changes affected this referral.

| Was it investigated, transferred, or declined? | Review and disposition records, transfer correspondence, and any communicated reasons |
| What evidence was reviewed? | Record requests, access applications, interview records, and references to the audio and exhibit |
| Did staffing or workload affect handling? | Case-specific assignment or priority records and the responsible reviewer’s explanation |

## What Would Falsify This

Confirmation of an investigation, transfer, or merits decision would change the account of the referral’s unresolved status. A complete audio record, court-file review, or independent witness evidence could corroborate or contradict the hearing sequence. Those findings should be assessed on their contents; the absence of communication to me does not establish the absence of agency action.
